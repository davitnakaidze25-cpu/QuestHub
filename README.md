🧭 Quest Hub
Quest Hub is a mobile well-being app based on book "Atomic Habits" by james clear that turns daily life into simple challenges and tasks. Users complete everyday goals, track progress, and compare achievements with friends and family to stay motivated and consistent.

🚀 Overview
Maintaining good habits is often hard without motivation. Quest Hub solves this by adding a social and gamified layer to well-being routines. It encourages users to complete small daily “quests” that improve productivity, health, and mindset.

✨ Features
🎯 Daily challenges and tasks
👥 Friend and family comparison system
📊 Progress tracking and streaks
🧠 Well-being focused goals (health, productivity, habits)
🔔 Reminders to stay consistent
📱 Concept

Quest Hub transforms personal development into a shared experience—making self-improvement more engaging through friendly competition and accountability.

🎯 Purpose
Encourage healthier daily habits
Increase motivation through social interaction
Make self-improvement consistent and fun
